Hi, I'm Harsh Lara. I lead and build AI systems end to end, across Google, Resolve AI, Microsoft, and Apple. I work the full generative AI stack: post-training, context, harness, and agent engineering, through to the outcomes it drives.

Today I lead AI research and experimentation at [Google](https://about.google/), building autonomous agents that go from raw security telemetry to a shipped infrastructure fix. The hard part is knowing when to trust them, so I own the harness as much as the model: sandboxed execution environments, online verifiers, and LLM user simulators that surface how agents fail before production does.

Before Google I was an early technical hire at [Resolve AI](https://resolve.ai/), where I helped take the company from seed to a $1.5B Series A. I architected the mitigations system, autonomous agents that resolve production incidents end to end, drove the launches that onboarded Coinbase, DoorDash, and MongoDB, and owned evaluation strategy across the agentic stack.

Earlier, as a Research Engineer on Gemini at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/), I led post-training tool-use for [Project Astra](https://deepmind.google/technologies/project-astra/) and Gemini Live, co-led MoDE (NAACL 2025), and contributed to OmegaPRM, which lifted Gemini's math reasoning from 51.0% to 69.4% on MATH500.

Before that I led AutoML at [Microsoft Azure ML](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/solutions/ai) and built on-device speech systems at [Apple Siri](https://www.apple.com/siri/). Master's from [Carnegie Mellon](https://www.cmu.edu/), Bachelor's from [NITK Surathkal](https://www.nitk.ac.in/). Four publications, three patents.
