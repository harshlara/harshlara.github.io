Compass

A few things I try to remember — for living, learning, and working.

Everyone needs a compass. This is mine — the handful of ideas I keep coming back to when I'm deciding, building, or just trying to live the days well. Not rules. Just reminders.

01

Keep Learning

Life moves in cycles. Every experience — good or rough — has something to teach.

  • Lead with curiosity → Learn for the wonder of it, not just the payoff.
  • Let outcomes guide, not define → Results are useful signals. They aren't a verdict on you.
  • Work in cycles → Try, get feedback, adjust, try again. Growth isn't a straight line.
  • Notice your patterns → Without awareness, the same mistakes loop. With it, you get to choose differently.
  • Honor beginnings and endings → Start things on purpose. End them on purpose too — letting go makes room for what's next.
02

Time Is the Real Currency

It's the one thing you can't get back. Treat it that way.

  • Structure makes room for play → Routines aren't cages. They free up space for spontaneity when it actually matters.
  • Time-box the effort → Decide how much something deserves. Give it everything inside that window, then step back.
  • Watch where it actually goes → Your calendar is a confession. Make sure it's the one you want to be making.
  • Choose with intent → Before yes, pause: is this the best use of this hour?
03

Lean Into the Hard Parts

Growth lives on the other side of discomfort, not around it.

  • Pick your hard → Every path costs something. Today's discomfort is usually cheaper than tomorrow's regret.
  • Push through the wall → The hardest stretch is often right before the reward.
  • Trust the compounding → What feels uphill at first becomes natural with reps.
  • Focus on the work, not the result → You control the showing up. The rest tends to itself.
04

Be Honest, First With Yourself

Growth starts the moment you stop pretending.

  • See yourself clearly → Strengths, gaps, blind spots — all of it. No flinching.
  • Self-honesty first → If you lie to yourself, nothing else holds together.
  • Tell the truth with care → Truth lands best with empathy. Be honest and kind, not one at the cost of the other.
  • Why it matters → Honesty builds trust, alignment, and freedom from the quiet exhaustion of pretending.
05

Just Start

Thinking matters. But nothing actually changes until you begin.

  • Don't overthink it → Clarity comes from doing. Planning is the warm-up, not the workout.
  • Start now, refine later → Movement makes momentum. Momentum makes everything else possible.
  • Don't tie yourself to one path → A life with range is a life that bends without breaking.
  • Play the long game → Mastery isn't fast. Showing up consistently is what compounds.
  • Earn the door opening → Opportunities follow effort. The more you build, the more shows up.
06

Take Joy Seriously

The work matters. So does everything around it.

  • Make room for play → Curiosity without a goal. Creativity without an output. The best ideas often show up here.
  • Don't postpone happiness → It isn't waiting at the finish line. It's woven through the days you actually live.
  • Protect what makes you smile → Silly hobbies, weird projects, friends who make you laugh. These aren't side dishes.
  • Stay light while doing hard things → Being serious is easy. Staying playful through the grind is the harder skill.
  • Build a life you'd want to live → Not one that just looks good from the outside. Your compass, your version of good.
